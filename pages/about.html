About Ryan Schmidt: Senior B2B SaaS Product Designer

✦ PRODUCT LEADERSHIP · FRONT-END CODE · SYSTEMS

Leadership

Good enterprise design happens in the details: database constraints, edge cases, and the exact spacing on the screen.

At i4cp, I am the only designer working alongside our VP of Product and four developers. My job covers the whole delivery cycle: interviewing members, writing requirements, maintaining our CSS tokens, and testing staging builds before code hits production. If a release looks clunky or breaks on smaller viewports, it stops with me.

Before this at D-Tools, I ran design reviews across five designers and six product managers. The best sessions were never the polite ones—they were the candid ones where an engineer caught an edge case we missed and saved us two weeks of rework.

Builder

I like building what I design. Working in the browser helps me test responsive layouts, keyboard interactions, and the details that are easy to miss in a static mockup.

I started writing code while designing web interfaces for school boards at TASB and transit apps at Bytemark.

These days, I use tools like Cursor and Claude Code to get working concepts into the browser in an afternoon. Prototyping in code lets me test how interactions actually feel, catch accessibility bugs early, and hand developers working markup instead of static pictures.

Experience

Six years designing software for people doing hard jobs, from warehouse dispatchers to HR executives.

I’ve designed software for school boards, city bus fleets, commercial AV integrators, and Fortune 500 HR executives.

My anthropology background taught me to observe how people actually work and listen for the workarounds they don't even think to mention. Flying as a naval aircrewman drilled in checklist discipline: when stakes are high, clear procedures keep you grounded. That combination keeps me focused on cutting through messy requirements to build tools that are calm, reliable, and hard to break.
