About Ryan Schmidt — Senior B2B SaaS Product Designer

Leadership

I care about how the whole product works, from the underlying logic to the details on screen.

At i4cp, I'm the sole product designer. I write PRDs, maintain design tokens, and work with our VP of Product and engineers to shape the roadmap. I also write Trello tickets and test work in staging, helping the team move from an idea to clear requirements and a release we've checked.

Before this at D-Tools, I facilitated twice-weekly design reviews with five designers and six PMs to stress-test assumptions before implementation. The best sessions were the ones where we uncovered hidden technical friction and shifted direction before writing code.

Builder

I like building what I design. Working in the browser helps me test responsive layouts, keyboard interactions, and the details that are easy to miss in a static mockup.

My coding background goes back nearly a decade, building responsive web interfaces for school board tools at TASB and transit ticketing apps at Bytemark.

I use Cursor and Claude Code to get working prototypes into the browser sooner. I still make the product decisions, review the output, and test how the experience behaves before engineering begins.
